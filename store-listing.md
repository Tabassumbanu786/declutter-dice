## Full description

Declutter Dice turns digital decluttering into a tiny daily habit.

Tap the die. Each face rolls a category — Inbox, Photos, Files, Apps,
Subscriptions, or a Wildcard surprise. You get one small, specific task,
like:

• Unsubscribe from one newsletter you never read
• Delete one blurry photo
• Cancel one subscription you forgot about

Do it, mark it done, and watch your streak grow.

WHY IT WORKS
Most decluttering apps ask for a big time commitment. Declutter Dice asks
for one tiny thing, once a day — the kind of task you can finish before
your tea gets cold.

FEATURES
• 72 real tasks across 6 categories
• Difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Brave) with XP for each one
• Daily streaks and levels, from Fresh Start to Declutter Master
• A full history of everything you've cleared
• Choose which categories show up
• Works completely offline — everything stays on your device, nothing is
  ever uploaded anywhere
