Privacy Policy

Declutter Dice · Last updated: 10 September 2026

Declutter Dice does not collect, store, or share any personal data on any server. Here is exactly how the app handles information:

What we store, and where

  • Your streak, XP, task history, and settings are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. We never see this data, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account system and no sign-in — the app doesn't know who you are.

Analytics and advertising

None. This app has no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind.

Third-party services

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which may log standard technical request information (such as IP address) as part of serving the font files. See Google's privacy policy for details. No other third-party service is used.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device settings, permanently deletes everything listed above. We have no way to recover it, because we never had a copy of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL.
